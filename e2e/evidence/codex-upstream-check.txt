## 3. The patched crate compiles

`cargo check -p codex-core` inside the full workspace never gets past dependency
resolution: it fetches the Chromium/V8 submodule chain
(`rusty_v8` → `chromium_build`, `icu`, `abseil-cpp`, `llvm-project`, …). That is
unrelated to `core` and does not finish on a constrained network.

Instead the check uses `e2e/probe/`: an out-of-workspace crate that depends on
the patched `codex-core` by path and reuses `codex-rs/Cargo.lock` plus the
workspace `[patch.crates-io]` table.
